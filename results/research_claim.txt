RESEARCH CLAIM
==============

Title (working):
  Gauge Selection as Symbolic Preconditioning for Rational Certificate Search
  in Hypergeometric Creative Telescoping

Central thesis:
  The bottleneck in WZ-style certificate search is not only solving the
  telescoping equation, but choosing a representation in which the rational
  certificate is low-complexity. Closed-form normalization (gauge selection)
  can change certificate search from unreachable to reachable under a fixed
  ansatz budget — acting as symbolic preconditioning.

Three empirical contributions:

  1. GAUGE SENSITIVITY
     Identical hypergeometric identities expressed in different gauge
     representations yield qualitatively different search difficulties.
     The closed-form gauge consistently makes the certificate reachable;
     raw and recurrence-LHS gauges fail within the same ansatz budget.

  2. STRUCTURAL GUIDED ANSATZ
     Structural features (predicted poles, recurrence order) reduce the
     denominator candidate space while preserving exact symbolic verification.
     Across r=1..4: guided search reduces runtime by nan%
     median (0 timed rows, 0 exactly verified certificates).

  3. SYMBOLIC SEARCH TRACE DATASET
     All search attempts (including failures) are recorded with:
     gauge, denominator candidates, systems tried, equations solved,
     runtime, and certificate degree. This negative-result corpus is
     rare in the creative telescoping literature.

Falsifiability test (weighted-binom r=1):
  Without closed-form gauge: all gauges fail (reachable=NO).
  After adding gauge G=k*C(n,k)/(n*2^(n-1)): reachable=YES, deg=1, 10 eqs, 0.03s.
  Runtime ratio (hardest/easiest gauge): ~60x.
